# BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis

**Project Domain:** Retail & Grocery Sales Analytics  
**Project Reference:** Sapphire IQ  
**Tools Used:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

## Project Overview

This project analyzes BlinkIT grocery sales data to understand product performance, outlet performance, sales patterns, customer ratings, and outlet locations.

Python is used to clean, explore, analyze, and visualize the available data to identify patterns and generate business insights that can support better decision-making.

## Objectives

1. **Understand Business and Sales Performance**
   - Analyze product categories and outlet types.
   - Calculate overall sales performance.
   - Examine customer ratings and product performance.

2. **Ensure Data Reliability**
   - Identify missing values.
   - Check for duplicate records.
   - Examine unusual values and potential outliers.
   - Standardize inconsistent data entries.

3. **Identify Factors Affecting Sales**
   - Compare sales across product categories.
   - Analyze sales by outlet type, size, and location.
   - Examine relationships between sales, item visibility, item weight, and customer ratings.
   - Identify high- and low-performing products and outlets.

4. **Support Business Decision-Making**
   - Identify products contributing most to sales.
   - Compare outlet performance.
   - Identify areas for improvement.
   - Provide practical recommendations to improve business performance.
